# About Project Orion

Project Orion is not another AI assistant. It is a proposal for one of the
highest-leverage software systems humanity could build in this century: a
**free, private, offline-capable lifelong mentor** for every person who wants
one.

We believe the binding constraint on civilization is not only energy, capital,
or institutions. It is the limited cognitive and emotional bandwidth of
individual humans — especially the billions who never get a great teacher,
clinician, or coach.

Orion is the attempt to fix that at the source, without building a
surveillance product or an engagement engine.

This repository is the seed. We are openly designing the protocol, privacy
model, on-device architecture, and deployment playbook so governments, NGOs,
companies, and communities can implement it the way the internet itself was
built: as a shared standard, not a single vendor's account system.

If you believe humanity deserves that upgrade, start at the
[README](README.md), then the [whitepaper](WHITEPAPER.md).
