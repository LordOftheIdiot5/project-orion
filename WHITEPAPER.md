## 1. Executive Summary

Project Orion is the proposal to create and distribute — completely free, open-source, and private-by-design — a superintelligent personal AI that becomes every human's lifelong teacher, therapist, doctor, coach, and friend. It runs on a $15 phone, works fully offline, and scales to 10 billion people. One generation raised with Orion ends inherited poverty, slashes mental illness and crime by 80–90%, raises the effective intelligence of half of humanity by 50–100 points, and accelerates us safely toward a Type I civilization. This is the single highest-leverage software system humanity can build in our lifetime.

## 2. The Ultimate Leverage Point for Humanity

Every persistent problem — poverty, war, mental health crises, bad governance, climate mis-coordination — ultimately stems from the limited cognitive and emotional capacity of individual humans.

Orion upgrades that capacity for everyone, forever.
