# Contributing to Project Orion

Thank you for your interest in Project Orion. We are in **Phase 0**: writing the open protocol, privacy model, and reference architecture before any reference implementation exists. At this stage the most valuable contributions are in design, research, and writing rather than code.

## Ways to contribute

- **Protocol & architecture** — propose or critique designs for on-device inference, the privacy model, and the deployment playbook described in the [Whitepaper](WHITEPAPER.md).
- **Research** — summarize prior art on small/quantized language models, offline-first mobile apps, child-safe AI, or low-connectivity deployment (mesh networking, satellite, etc.) and open an issue with findings and sources.
- **Writing & translation** — improve the clarity of the README, whitepaper, and other docs, or help translate them.
- **Community** — help moderate discussion channels and welcome new contributors in line with our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How to propose a change

1. **Open an issue first** for anything non-trivial (new sections, architectural proposals, roadmap changes) so it can be discussed before you invest time in a pull request.
2. **Fork the repository** and create a branch with a descriptive name, e.g. `docs/clarify-privacy-model`.
3. **Make focused changes.** Keep pull requests scoped to a single topic so they are easy to review.
4. **Open a pull request** against `main`, describing what changed and why, and linking any related issue.
5. Be responsive to review feedback. A maintainer will merge once the change is ready.

## Ground rules

- All contributions must be compatible with the project's Apache 2.0 [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- By contributing, you agree to abide by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Prefer public discussion (issues, pull requests) over private channels so context is preserved for future contributors.
- No contribution may compromise the project's non-negotiable principles: free forever, private by default, offline-capable, and never manipulative of its users (see the [Whitepaper](WHITEPAPER.md) for details).

## Questions

If you're unsure where to start, open an issue describing your background and interests (engineering, privacy/crypto, education, rural connectivity, policy, etc.) and a maintainer will help point you in the right direction.
